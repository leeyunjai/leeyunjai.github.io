On September 1, LG and Samsung each launched a budget 14-inch Windows laptop in Korea. Both use Intel's new Core Series 3 chips, both target students and first-time buyers, and both sit in the 1.2 to 1.5 million KRW band. That makes them a natural head-to-head. This piece compares what has been officially published so far, and is honest about what has not.

## LG gram Book AI 2026 14 (14U40V)

The gram Book line is LG's cheaper sibling to the flagship gram. Until now it only came in 15 and 16-inch sizes, so a 14-inch model is a real change for the line, not a refresh.

| Item | LG gram Book AI 2026 14 |
|---|---|
| CPU | Intel Core Series 3 (Wildcat Lake) |
| Display | 14-inch. Resolution and panel type: TBC |
| RAM / SSD | TBC for the 14-inch model. LG's store lists the 15-inch gram Book AI 2026 at 8 or 16 GB RAM and 256 or 512 GB NVMe with one spare PCIe 4.0 slot; expect similar tiers |
| Battery | 61.2 Wh, up to 30.5 hours by LG's measurement |
| Weight / thickness | 1.29 kg / 14.9 mm, aluminum body |
| Ports | HDMI, USB-A, USB-C, headphone jack, wired LAN (RJ-45) |
| Price | from 1,450,000 KRW |
| Availability | on sale Sep 7 at LGE.com and online malls; LG Best Shop from mid-September |

What stands out is the battery and the ports. A 61.2 Wh pack in a 1.29 kg body is a flagship-class ratio at a budget price. A built-in RJ-45 jack is rare on a 14-inch laptop in 2026 and will matter to anyone who plugs into a lab or school network. The gap is the display: LG has not published resolution, panel type, or brightness, and those decide whether 1,450,000 KRW is fair.

## Samsung New Galaxy Book6

Samsung's entry is a single-configuration budget model under the "New" prefix, separate from the Galaxy Book6 that launched in April. One size, one color, a lower entry price.

| Item | Samsung New Galaxy Book6 |
|---|---|
| CPU | Intel Core Series 3 |
| Display | 14-inch (35.6 cm), 16:10 anti-glare. Samsung's Galaxy Book6 page lists WUXGA (1920 x 1200) at up to 350 nits; whether the "New" model uses the same panel: TBC |
| RAM / SSD | TBC (varies by configuration) |
| Battery | up to 25 hours of video playback; 30-minute charge restores up to 33% |
| Weight | 1.35 kg |
| Ports | 2x USB-C, 1x USB-A, HDMI, headphone/mic jack |
| Price | 1,190,000 to 1,490,000 KRW depending on CPU and OS |
| Availability | Sep 1 at Samsung Stores, Samsung.com, and open markets |

Samsung's advantage is the entry price and immediate availability. 1,190,000 KRW is 260,000 KRW below LG's floor, and the machine is in stores today. It gives up 60 grams and wired LAN, and Samsung has not published the battery capacity in Wh, only a playback-hours figure, so the two battery claims cannot be compared directly.

## Head to head

| | LG gram Book AI 2026 14 | Samsung New Galaxy Book6 |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | 1.29 kg | 1.35 kg |
| Battery | 61.2 Wh, 30.5 h claimed | Wh TBC, 25 h video claimed |
| Wired LAN | Yes | No |
| USB-C count | 1 (TBC) | 2 |
| Entry price | 1,450,000 KRW | 1,190,000 KRW |
| On sale | Sep 7 | Sep 1 |
| Korea availability | Yes | Yes |

**Pick the LG** if you carry the laptop all day and want the longest battery and a LAN port. Robotics and embedded classes that still use wired networks will appreciate not needing a dongle.

**Pick the Samsung** if price is the deciding factor or you need the laptop this week. Two USB-C ports also make it easier to charge and connect a dev board at the same time.

## Verdict

Neither company has published a full spec sheet yet, and the display is the missing piece on both. If LG's 14-inch panel turns out to be a 1920 x 1200 IPS like the 15-inch model, the extra 260,000 KRW buys you a bigger battery, a lighter body, and a LAN port, which is a fair trade for a student machine. If Samsung's "New" model keeps the 350-nit WUXGA panel of the regular Book6, it is the safer budget pick. I would wait for the retail listings around September 7 before ordering either.
